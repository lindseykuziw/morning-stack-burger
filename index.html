Meet The Morning Stack

Breakfast done better

Discover the burger

 Built for better mornings

A great breakfast starts with fresh, reliable ingredients and careful preparation. The Morning Stack combines breakfast flavours in one hearty burger, bringing together ingredients that create a satisfying mix of taste and texture. Each layer adds something different, from the savoury burger patty to the breakfast-inspired toppings. The goal is to create a burger that feels familiar while still offering something different from a traditional morning meal. With bold flavours, filling ingredients, and a stacked presentation, the Morning Stack is designed to stand out as a memorable breakfast burger that can be enjoyed on a morning or a relaxed weekend.

Savoury Morning Stack

The Savoury Morning Stack is a hearty burger that brings together a range of classic breakfast items. It consists of bacon, an egg, melted cheese, tomato, and a creamy sauce, all of which are placed on a toasted bun to create a variety of savoury flavours and textures. The soft egg and cheese provide a contrast to the crisp bacon, while the tomato adds a fresh element. Since each ingredient has been selected for a particular reason, this breakfast burger is not only familiar but also more satisfying than a usual breakfast sandwich.

Stacked to Satisfy

A breakfast burger is not simply a collection of ingredients; the toasted bun holds all the components in place, the patty and the egg give it a satisfying quality, bacon provides a crisp element, and the tomato offsets the flavours. Once all these parts are put together, each bite has the right taste.

A Bite of Breakfast Burger History

The idea of the breakfast burger came about when cooks mixed diner-style burgers with traditional breakfast sandwiches by including eggs, bacon, cheese, and breakfast sauces, which made the burgers very popular at brunch times. Afterwards, various restaurants experimented with new ingredients such as hash browns, avocado, maple flavouring, and spicy sauces. As time went on, breakfast burgers changed because cooks carried out experiments and tastes evolved, eventually combining two favourite meals into a single popular dish.

 What is a Breakfast Burger?

A breakfast burger features the basic structure of a standard burger together with items that are typically served for breakfast. Typical additions are eggs, bacon, cheese, hash browns, tomato, and savoury sauces, all of which are placed on a bun with a burger patty. Knowing how these ingredients complement one another enables one to prepare a well-balanced meal that offers a variety of flavours and textures. As time has gone on, cooks have come up with a great many variations, displaying both their experience and creativity in mixing popular breakfast foods with the traditional burger style and exceptional taste and flavour.

Discover The Breakfast Burger 

The Morning Stack is a breakfast burger built around familiar morning favourites and traditions. It combines a savoury burger patty with egg, bacon, cheese, tomato, and other breakfast-inspired toppings in one stacked meal. The idea comes from combining the comfort of a traditional breakfast sandwich with the size and flavour of a burger. Each ingredient adds a different taste or texture, creating a meal that feels both familiar and different. The Morning Stack shows how breakfast foods can be reimagined into a creative burger that is filling, flavourful, and designed for anyone looking for something more substantial in the morning.

Where It Started

Breakfast burgers developed out of two well-known choices: the traditional diner burger and the breakfast sandwich. Gradually, cooks started to put together beef patties​ with eggs, bacon, and cheese. By repeatedly trying out and improving these combinations, they transformed a basic concept into the substantial breakfast burger which we now know.

The Modern Stack

The breakfast burger nowadays goes much further than the simple bacon-and-egg mix. In order to produce new flavours and textures cooks make use of things like hash browns, avocado, spicy sauces, and various kinds of cheese. Because of years of trying out different combinations the breakfast burger has become a creative and customizable dish.

Breakfast Goes Handheld

Breakfast sandwiches made it easier to enjoy morning meals when on the move since eggs, cheese, and bacon could all be placed inside a bun and eaten without the need for a plate. When cooks began to try out bigger portions and new ingredients, the handheld breakfast items thus inspired more substantial dishes, such as the modern breakfast burger.
